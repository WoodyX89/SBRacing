OUR STORY

Born in the mud.
Built for the trails.

SB Racing (Soggy Bottom Racing) started in 2024 when a group of shift workers from CF Industries in Medicine Hat needed an outlet after long days at the plant. What began as after-work rides through the coulees quickly became something bigger.

We ride year-round in the stunning badlands and prairie trails surrounding Medicine Hat — from technical coulee singletrack to high-speed flow trails in Cypress Hills. We welcome riders of all abilities and backgrounds.

Community First
No ego. Just good people on bikes.
All Levels Welcome
Beginner clinics to expert epics.
THE SB DIFFERENCE
  • Weekly group rides every Tuesday & Thursday evening + weekend adventures
  • Private Discord with trail reports, weather alerts & ride planning
  • Skills clinics, bike maintenance workshops & youth programs
  • Annual Soggy Bottom Wind-Up: BBQ, race, and awards night
  • Strong ties to local trails advocacy and land access
